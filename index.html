THE CHALLENGE

What if the person trusted with the paper is the risk?

Exam leaks can happen before an exam begins. An insider may copy, share, or photograph a paper, making trust in one person an incomplete safeguard.

That risk is known as an insider threat. ExamShield AI explores how independent checks and traceable handling can reduce opportunities for leaks and improve accountability.

!

Security is a process, not a promise.
No single tool can guarantee that a determined insider will never disclose information.

OUR APPROACH

More than one set of eyes.

A proposed multi-level authorization workflow puts checks around the paper, its access, and its release.

01
✎

Create securely

The paper setter prepares the exam paper in a controlled environment.

02
⌑

Protect & identify

The proposal uses encryption and a unique document identifier to support tracking.

03
◎

Independent approval

A separate examination controller reviews and authorizes release. The creator cannot approve alone.

04
⌁

Log & release

Access is recorded, unusual activity can be reviewed, and release follows the approved time.

!
What about a secret photograph?

A camera can bypass digital controls. Controlled viewing, supervision, restricted copying, and individually watermarked copies can reduce risk and support investigation—but cannot make photography impossible.

A prototype with honest limits

Exact matching only catches unchanged files. The extension cannot stop screenshots or photos from another device. Encryption, identity checks, enforced two-person release, supervision, and tamper-resistant audit systems need institutional infrastructure. This project demonstrates concepts; it is not a complete exam-security system.
